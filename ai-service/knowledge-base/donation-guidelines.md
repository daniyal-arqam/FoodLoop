# Donation guidelines for FoodLoop listings

Donation on FoodLoop means creating an Available listing so a verified community organization can claim and collect the food. List early, as soon as surplus volume is known, rather than waiting until closing.

Include the food name, category, quantity, pickup address, available window, and expiry time. Do not list food that staff would not eat themselves under the same conditions.

Organizations must be verified before they can claim. Providers should keep the listing accurate if quantity changes. After a claim, the listing becomes Reserved until collection is recorded.

FoodLoop is a coordination tool. It does not replace the provider's donation policy, insurance, or local charitable-food rules. If a donation policy forbids a category, do not list that category even if it is technically edible.
