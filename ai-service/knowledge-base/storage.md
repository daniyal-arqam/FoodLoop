# Storage and holding of surplus food

Keep surplus food in the same storage conditions used for food that would still be sold or served. Cold items stay refrigerated. Frozen items stay frozen until pickup. Hot prepared food should be listed only if it can be collected quickly and kept in a covered, labeled container.

Label each tray or package with the food name, the time it was prepared or pulled from service, and any allergen information the provider already uses. Store surplus separately from waste and from chemicals.

Dairy, meat, seafood, and cooked rice or pasta should not sit unrefrigerated while waiting for a claim. If pickup cannot happen while the food is still under proper holding, expire or withdraw the listing instead of stretching storage time.

Shelf-stable canned or sealed bakery items can wait longer, but damaged packaging, swollen cans, or foods past the provider's own use-by practice should not be donated.
