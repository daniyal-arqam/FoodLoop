# Sustainability guidelines for food rescue

FoodLoop exists to keep edible surplus in the human food chain. Coordination (listing, matching, claim, collection) reduces waste only when pickup happens before expiry.

Prefer same-day or next-service redistribution for prepared food. Longer storage is appropriate for sealed bakery or produce when holding conditions are already documented.

Estimate impact from collected listings, not from food that expired on the platform. Expired listings are a signal to list earlier or to match closer organizations, not a sustainability success.

FoodLoop does not process payments. Sustainability here means fewer edible portions discarded and fewer unnecessary extra batches, not carbon accounting from a laboratory model. Dashboard CO₂ and water figures are heuristics from collected quantity.
