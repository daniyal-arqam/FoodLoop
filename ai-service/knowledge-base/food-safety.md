# Food safety basics for surplus handling

FoodLoop does not certify that food is safe. Providers remain responsible for following their local food-safety rules and their own kitchen procedures.

Before a listing is created, staff should confirm the food was held under their normal temperature controls, was not served to guests in a way that allows contamination, and still looks and smells as expected. If those checks cannot be confirmed from available information, the food should not be listed.

Prepared foods, dairy, meat, and cooked leftovers need extra caution. If a product has been left in the temperature danger zone, mixed with unknown leftovers, or shows spoilage, it is not suitable for redistribution on FoodLoop.

Do not provide home-canning, raw-milk, or other high-risk handling advice through the platform. When in doubt, keep the item out of the surplus listing and dispose of it according to the provider's waste procedure.
