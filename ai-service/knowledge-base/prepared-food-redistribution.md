# Redistributing prepared food

Before redistributing prepared food, confirm three things: it was cooked and held under the provider's normal controls, it can be collected within the available window, and the receiving organization can use that category and quantity.

Prepared meals should be listed with a short pickup window. Share allergen notes the kitchen already records. Do not reheat, recook, or mix leftover pans into a new dish for donation unless that is already part of the provider's documented process.

Community organizations should claim only what they can collect and serve promptly. After pickup, record collection in FoodLoop so the listing moves from Reserved to Collected.

If holding time, ingredients, or temperature history is unknown, do not redistribute the prepared food. The knowledge base does not replace a food-safety officer or local health department.
